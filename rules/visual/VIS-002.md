## Rule

Use a `*-foreground` token only on its own fill, `border-input` for the edges of controls, and a state colour such as `ring`, `destructive` or `accent` only for its state. Never use raw palette classes such as `text-gray-500` for something that carries meaning.

## Why

A token is a promise about where a colour goes. `primary-foreground` was chosen to be read on `primary`, `input` to mark the edge of a field, `ring` to show focus. Justified's contrast test checks those pairs in both themes, and a theme change reaches every place that uses them by name. A colour borrowed for another job keeps none of that. It was never measured where it now sits, it does not follow the theme, and when the palette changes it breaks without anyone noticing. A colour that has one meaning also keeps that meaning: once red marks a sale price, it no longer warns of an error. No WCAG criterion asks for tokens. What the law asks is the measured result, in VIS-001 for text and VIS-004 for borders and graphics. Using colours by role is how the judges keep that result true across themes and later changes, and it keeps colour cues readable in the sense of [WCAG 2.2, 1.4.1 Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#use-of-color). Following the pairs does not prove contrast on its own; only measuring does.

## Do

- Pair each `*-foreground` with its own fill: `text-card-foreground` on `bg-card`, `text-popover-foreground` on `bg-popover`, `text-primary-foreground` on `bg-primary`. `foreground` and `muted-foreground` go on `background`, and `muted-foreground` also on `muted`.
- Use the status colours as Justified tests them: `text-destructive`, `text-success`, `text-warning` and `text-info` as text on the page, and each status fill under its own `*-foreground`. An order in a plant nursery's shop that could not be charged reads "Payment failed" in `text-destructive`.
- Edge every field with `border-input`: an `Input`, a custom select, a combobox trigger, a date picker button. Their edge is the only sign of where they are. Keep `border-border` for dividers and the edge of a `Card`, where nothing depends on seeing it.
- Keep `ring` for the focus indicator that KBD-001 describes. Show a chosen plan or an active tab some other way, as VIS-003a says.
- Keep `bg-accent` for items that are hovered, highlighted, pressed or selected, which is how shadcn's menus and `Toggle` use it. Give a static panel `bg-muted` or `bg-card`, so an item that turns `bg-accent` inside it still shows.
- When the product needs a colour it does not have, such as a soft badge of `bg-primary/10` with `text-primary` for *Ships in 2 days*, add it to the tokens with a name for its role and add the pair to the contrast test, so it is measured in both themes. This step is our own choice.
- Give brand colours the same treatment: a token with a role and a tested pair, not a one-off class.
- Treat an inverse surface, such as a dark tooltip in the light theme, as a pair like any other.

## Don't

- Use raw palette classes such as `text-gray-500`, `bg-slate-100`, `text-blue-600` or `border-gray-200`, or a hex value in `style`. Such a colour has no role, so nothing says where it may go. It does not follow the theme, and nobody has tested it.
- Put a foreground on someone else's fill: `text-primary-foreground` on `bg-muted`, `text-muted-foreground` on `bg-primary`, `text-accent-foreground` on `bg-destructive`. Each is tested only against its own partner.
- Use `text-destructive` for a sale price or a *New* label, `bg-success` as a brand accent, or `text-warning` for a heading. DLG-005 keeps the destructive button for actions that destroy something.
- Mark a selected card with `border-ring` or `ring-2 ring-ring`. Selection then looks like focus.
- Draw an `Input` override, a custom select or a combobox trigger with `border-border`. It measures about 1.4:1 against the light page and 1.5:1 against the dark one, made for a divider, not for the only edge of a control; VIS-004 gives the law's side.
- Mix systems in one pair, as in `bg-card dark:bg-zinc-900`. The pair now depends on two palettes, and the test sees only one.
- Reuse chart colours for status, or status colours for chart series in a chart that is not about status. shadcn's `--chart-1` to `--chart-5` are a role of their own; Justified does not define or test them.

## Exceptions

- **Colours that carry nothing.** An illustration or a photo is outside the rule. A colour that people read, or that marks a control, a state or a boundary, is inside it.
- **Neighbouring rules.** The contrast a pair must reach is VIS-001 for text and VIS-004 for borders and graphics. The second cue next to a colour is VIS-003a. The focus ring itself is KBD-001.
