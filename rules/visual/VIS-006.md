## Rule

When the person's device or browser asks for reduced motion, replace movement across the screen, such as sliding, zooming, scaling, parallax and bouncing, with a short fade or an instant change. Stop motion that only decorates and show its final state. Keep motion that is essential.

## Why

For some people, things that slide, zoom or drift on a screen bring on dizziness, nausea or a migraine that can last the rest of the day. They tell their system once, with a setting such as Reduce Motion on Apple devices or the animation switch in Windows and Android, and the browser passes that on to every page as `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. A page that ignores it makes them pay for its effects. Asking for less motion is not asking for none: a fade in place does not move anything across the view, and it still shows that something changed, which a hard cut can hide. No Level AA criterion requires this, so the rule rests on the judges. [WCAG 2.2, 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#animation-from-interactions) asks for it at Level AAA. That is a further aim, not a requirement here. A control that lets everyone pause moving content is VIS-005, and keeping meaning when motion goes is VIS-007.

## Do

- Put one rule in the base stylesheet under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` that turns off movement and keeps opacity. With `tw-animate-css`, reset its enter and exit variables rather than setting `transform: none` or `animation: none`, which would also stop transforms the layout needs or remove the fades: `--tw-enter-translate-x`, `--tw-enter-translate-y` and `--tw-enter-rotate` to `0`, `--tw-enter-scale` to `1`, and the same for the `--tw-exit-*` set. shadcn's `Dialog`, `Popover` and `DropdownMenu` then fade in place, and a `Sheet` fades in instead of sliding the width of the screen.
- In your own components, pair every movement class from `tw-animate-css` or `tailwindcss-animate` with a reduced form, for example `animate-in fade-in motion-safe:slide-in-from-bottom-4`, so the panel fades in place when the setting is on.
- With framer-motion or Motion, wrap the app in `<MotionConfig reducedMotion="user">`. It then drops transform and layout animation and keeps fades and colour changes. Use `useReducedMotion()` where you build your own variants, such as an `AnimatePresence` page transition that slides in with `initial={{ x: 300 }}`.
- Turn off parallax made with `useScroll` and `useTransform`, and slide-ups on `whileInView`. The content is simply in place.
- Stop decorative loops, floating cards, animated gradients and digits that roll up to a number, and show the end state at once.
- Our own reading, since no judge names it: make scrolling instant under reduced motion. Write `motion-safe:scroll-smooth` instead of `scroll-smooth`, and pass `behavior: "auto"` to `scrollIntoView` when the setting is on.
- Read the setting with a listener, not once on mount, so a change takes effect without a reload. On the server the setting is unknown, so start from the reduced form or from CSS, not from a guess in script.
- End every reduced animation on its final, visible state. A card that waits at `opacity-0` for a keyframe must still appear when the keyframe is off.
- Keep essential motion, in a calmer form if you can. A loader still shows that work goes on, as STA-001 asks; a slower spin or a static bar with "Loading invoices" is fine. A video the person chose to play still plays.
- Check that a `Dialog` still closes and leaves the page when its exit animation is cut, and that focus moves as DLG-006 asks without waiting for any animation.

## Don't

- Use `animate-in slide-in-from-right zoom-in-95` in a custom panel with no `motion-reduce:` or `motion-safe:` variant.
- Leave `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` out, so every `motion.div` in the app slides and springs whatever the person has set.
- Set `scroll-smooth` on `html` and smooth-scroll to every anchor for everyone.
- Paste a snippet that sets every duration to almost zero, so the fades the setting allows go too and content jumps.
- Hide content with `opacity-0` until an animation that is now off reveals it, so it never appears.
- Treat the setting as the pause control for content that moves by itself. That control is VIS-005, for everyone.

## Exceptions

- **Motion the person drives.** Content that moves because the person scrolls, or a card that follows their finger or pointer in a drag, is their own movement and stays. Motion added on top of scrolling, such as parallax, is not.
- **Essential motion.** Motion that is the content itself, such as a video the person started or an animation being edited, stays.
- **A product's own switch.** A motion setting inside the product, on top of the system one, is welcome but not required by this rule.
