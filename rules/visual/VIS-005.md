## Rule

When something starts by itself and shares the screen with other content, give a way to pause, stop or hide it if it moves, blinks or scrolls for more than five seconds. If it updates by itself, such as an auto-playing carousel, give that way, or a way to set how often, at any length. Essential motion is exempt.

## Why

Motion pulls the eye whether the person wants it to or not. A logo strip that never stops, a slide that changes while someone is still reading it, or a card that floats up and down beside a form makes it hard to read and hard to think, most of all for people with attention or reading difficulties and for people who use a screen magnifier, who may see little else. [WCAG 2.2, 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#pause-stop-hide) requires the way out. It is Level A, so it sits inside AA. It has two parts. Moving, blinking and scrolling need a control only when all three hold: it starts by itself, it lasts more than five seconds and other content is on screen with it. Content that updates on its own needs a control however long it runs, or a way to set how often it updates. The criterion covers every part of the page, decoration included.

The Working Group's Understanding page for 2.2.2 counts motion that starts on hover, on focus or when scrolled into view as starting by itself; only a deliberate press does not. Its techniques let the person pause and resume from the same place, and list a scrolling strip with no pause as a failure ([G4](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G4), [F16](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F16)). These are the Working Group's reading of the criterion, not its text. Reduced motion is a separate rule, VIS-006.

## Do

- A pricing page has a shadcn `Carousel` of customer quotes with `plugins={[Autoplay({ delay: 5000 })]}` from `embla-carousel-autoplay` 8, the version shadcn's `Carousel` uses. Before the slides in reading order sits a button *Pause quotes*. Pressed, it calls the plugin's `stop()`, its label becomes *Play quotes*, and the carousel stays still until the person presses it again. The label follows the plugin's state through `emblaApi.on("autoplay:stop")` and `emblaApi.on("autoplay:play")`, so it is right also when a drag has stopped the autoplay. The button can be reached with Tab and shows focus (KBD-001).
- Our own reading of the criterion, as the safest one: make the control a visible button that keeps its state. `stopOnMouseEnter`, `stopOnFocusIn` and `stopOnInteraction` are welcome as extras, but none is a control the person can find and see the state of, and with `stopOnInteraction: false` the first two start the motion again as soon as the pointer or focus leaves. The `CarouselPrevious` and `CarouselNext` buttons are not a pause either.
- A logo wall under the hero runs as `animate-[marquee_40s_linear_infinite]`. A small button *Pause logos* next to it sets `animation-play-state: paused`, so the strip stops where it is and starts again from there.
- A hero background `<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline>` gets a visible button *Pause video* over its corner. Or it plays once, stops within five seconds and leaves its last frame.
- A "New" badge with `animate-pulse [animation-iteration-count:2]` pulses twice, for four seconds, and then stays still. That ends inside five seconds, so it needs no control. `animate-pulse` alone loops forever.
- A framer-motion card that floats with `animate={{ y: [0, -8, 0] }}` and `transition={{ repeat: Infinity }}` either stops within five seconds or is covered by the page's motion control.
- A dashboard whose numbers refresh every 30 seconds has a switch *Update automatically*, on by default, with its label visible beside it (FRM-001). Turned off, the numbers hold until the person presses *Refresh now*. A select for how often it updates also meets the criterion.
- One control for the whole page that stops every moving part is allowed, and is kinder than many small ones when a page has several. Our own advice: put it early, near the top, so a keyboard user meets it before the motion.
- Hiding passes too: a *Close* on an animated promotion banner that removes it meets the criterion.
- Our own advice, beyond the requirement: do not auto-advance a carousel at all. Let the slides change only when the person presses an arrow or swipes. Then this rule does not apply, and people can read each slide for as long as they need.

## Don't

- Add Embla `Autoplay` to a shadcn `Carousel` and rely on `stopOnMouseEnter` and `stopOnFocusIn`. Someone reading from a distance, using a magnifier or not yet at the carousel has nothing to press, and nothing shows that it can be stopped.
- Rotate testimonials, tips or headlines on a `setInterval` with no pause.
- Run an animated gradient (`animate-gradient` on `bg-[length:200%_200%]`), a particle background or an `animate-spin` blob behind the content forever, on the grounds that it is only decoration.
- Start a looping marquee with `whileInView` and `repeat: Infinity` when it scrolls into view, with no control. Scrolling it into view does not count as asking for it.
- Pause the motion only while it has focus and start it again as soon as focus leaves.
- Our own reading: do not treat `motion-reduce:animate-none` as the control. Many people who are bothered by motion never turn that setting on. Honour it as VIS-006 asks, and give the control as well.

## Exceptions

- **Short motion.** Moving, blinking or scrolling that starts by itself and stops within five seconds needs no control. This allowance does not cover content that updates on a timer.
- **Started by the person.** A video the person pressed play on, a carousel that moves only when its arrows are pressed or it is swiped, and an update the person asked for with *Refresh now* do not start by themselves.
- **Essential motion.** Motion is essential when taking it away would change what the content is, such as an animation the person is editing in an animation tool. Decoration is never essential.
- **Nothing else on screen.** Motion that is the only thing on screen, with nothing else to read or use, is outside the criterion. In a shadcn app that is rare beyond the very first load.
- **Loaders.** Spinners, progress bars and skeletons are STA-001's, including how 2.2.2 applies to them. A spinner that starts because the person pressed a button does not start by itself.
- **Flashing.** Rapid flashing, above three flashes within one second, is a matter for [WCAG 2.2, 2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#three-flashes-or-below-threshold). A pause button does not make such flashing acceptable, because harm can come before anyone presses it.
