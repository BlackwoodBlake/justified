## Rule

While something is being dragged, mark on screen the place that would receive it if it were let go now. Remove the mark as soon as the dragged thing leaves that place, is dropped or the drag is cancelled.

## Why

A drag is a promise made in advance: let go here and this will happen. Without a mark on the landing place, people let go and then look to see what they did, and a row in the wrong slot or a file that missed the zone is found only afterwards, if at all. A mark that stays after the item has moved on is worse, because it points at a place that will not get the drop. No WCAG criterion asks for a drop mark. Once there is one, [WCAG 2.2, 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#non-text-contrast) asks 3:1 for the part people need to see it, and [1.4.1 Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#use-of-color) rules out a change of hue as the only sign. The mark is for people who can see the screen; what assistive technology hears is ACT-007b.

## Do

- In a sortable list *Reading list* built with dnd-kit, read the sortable `over` id and draw a 2px line in the gap where the row will go. Do not rely on rows shuffling through `transform` alone, which shows nothing when the layout does not move them.
- On a board, read `isOver` from `useDroppable` on each column and give the column under the card a thicker border and a light fill.
- With nested targets, such as a card inside a column, mark only the one that would take the drop.
- In a react-dropzone area, change the zone while a file is over it, using `isDragActive`: a tinted fill with an upload icon and the text "Release to upload".
- Clear the mark in `onDragEnd` and `onDragCancel`, so it is gone after a drop and after Escape.
- Show the same mark during a keyboard move with dnd-kit's `KeyboardSensor`. Sighted keyboard users need it too.
- Keep the drag-over mark distinct from the focus ring on a zone that can also be clicked (ACT-007a). They are different states; KBD-001 owns the ring.
- Our own choice, beyond the rule: give the held item a raised look or a lighter copy that follows the pointer; show no mark, or a "not allowed" sign, over a place that cannot take the item; and on touch, draw the line ahead of the finger rather than under it.
- When saving after a drop fails, FBK-002 applies. Our own choice: do not let the item snap back without a word. While a save after the drop is under way, a placeholder in the new spot follows STA-001 and STA-005a.

## Don't

- Let a column stay highlighted after the card has been carried on to the next one.
- Mark a column and the card inside it at once, so it is unclear which one takes the drop.
- Change only the hue of the zone's border, from one colour to another of about the same lightness, with no change in weight or fill. Without a 3:1 lightness difference, colour is the only sign (VIS-003a).
- Leave a drop zone looking the same whether or not a file is over it.
- Keep the mark on screen after the person pressed Escape.

## Exceptions

- **No drag.** A place that is chosen by pressing, such as the *Move to* menu in ACT-007a, needs no drop mark.
- **Neighbouring rules.** The way without dragging is ACT-007a, the words for assistive technology are ACT-007b, and the focus ring is KBD-001.
