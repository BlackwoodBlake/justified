# Contributing

Thank you for taking an interest in Justified by Blackwood & Blake.

## Issues are welcome

Open an issue if you find a bug, a broken or outdated source link, unclear wording or a rule that does not work in practice.

Objections to a verdict are especially welcome. If you think a rule gets a source's stance wrong, or that the verdict should be different, open an issue and include:

- the rule it concerns
- what you think is wrong, and why
- a link to the current official page that supports your view

An objection is treated as an appeal: the rule is reviewed again, and the outcome is recorded with its reasons.

## Pull requests are not accepted

Every rule text is written and reviewed through a fixed process (see [WRITING.md](WRITING.md)), so pull requests are closed without review. Open an issue instead, and the change will be made through that process.

No sponsor, donor or client can influence a verdict.
